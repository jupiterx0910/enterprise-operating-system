# Business × People Integration / 管事 × 管人贯通

## Core thesis / 核心命题

An enterprise operating system cannot manage business and people as two disconnected systems.

企业经营系统不能把“业务”和“人”拆成两套彼此独立的系统。

The operating spine is:

> **Manage the work × Manage the people, connected by mechanisms.**
>
> **管事 × 管人，以机制贯通。**

`管事` answers: What outcome must be achieved, through what work, decisions, resources, and cadence?

`管人` answers: What roles, capabilities, responsibilities, development, evaluation, and allocation are required to achieve it?

`机制` connects the two through decision rights, processes, resources, information, incentives, governance, and review.

---

## Five-stage operating spine / 五阶段经营主轴

| Business track / 管事 | People track / 管人 | Integration question / 贯通问题 |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Market insight & strategic direction / 市场洞察与战略方向** | **Organization inventory & formation / 组织盘点与阵型设计** | What organization and capabilities does the strategy require? / 战略需要什么组织与能力？ |
| **2. Metrics & task decomposition / 指标设定与任务拆解** | **Accountability definition & talent matching / 权责定义与人才匹配** | Who owns which outcome, with what authority and resources? / 谁对什么结果负责，拥有什么权力与资源？ |
| **3. Execution & task advancement / 过程执行与任务推进** | **Coaching & resource enablement / 过程教练与资源赋能** | Is the issue capability, coordination, resources, mechanism, or execution? / 问题究竟来自能力、协同、资源、机制还是执行？ |
| **4. Business assessment / 业务考核** | **Performance evaluation & value distribution / 绩效评价与价值分配** | Are rewards and consequences aligned with real value creation? / 评价与分配是否真正对应价值创造？ |
| **5. Business review / 业务复盘** | **Organization review & asset accumulation / 组织复盘与资产沉淀** | What should change in strategy, organization, talent, mechanism, and institutional memory? / 战略、组织、人才、机制和组织记忆应该沉淀或改变什么？ |

The two tracks should move in the same operating cadence. A business review without organization implications is incomplete; a talent review without business context is also incomplete.

两条线应运行在同一个经营节奏中。没有组织影响的业务复盘是不完整的；脱离业务上下文的人才盘点同样是不完整的。

---

## Three-line integration / 三线贯通

For AI-native operation, use three interlocked lines:

```text
BUSINESS / 业务线
Outcome → Strategy → Work → Metrics → Review

PEOPLE / 人才线
Capabilities → Roles → Accountability → Talent → Development / Allocation

MECHANISM / 机制线
Decision rights → Process → Resources → Information → Incentives → Governance
```

The mechanism line is not a third silo. It is the connective tissue that makes business and people mutually executable.

机制不是第三个孤岛，而是让“管事”和“管人”真正互相可执行的连接组织。

---

## Strategy-to-organization chain / 战略到组织的推导链

```text
Strategy
→ Must-win priorities
→ Critical capabilities
→ Critical workflows
→ Decision rights
→ Accountabilities
→ Roles
→ Talent
→ Goals
→ Resources
→ Incentives
→ Operating cadence
```

Do not start organization design from an org chart. Start from the work and capabilities required by strategy.

不要从组织架构图开始做组织设计，要从战略要求的工作与能力开始。

---

## Business-to-people causality / 从业务到人的因果链

A people intervention is valid only when the link from business outcome to role requirement to person evidence is explicit.

只有当“经营结果 → 岗位要求 → 人因证据”的链条清楚时，人才干预才成立。

```text
Business result
→ work requirement
→ role accountability
→ capability requirement
→ observed person evidence
→ talent decision
```

This prevents two common errors:

1. using people as the default explanation for system failures;
2. using HR processes that are disconnected from business outcomes.

这可以防止两个常见错误：

1. 把系统问题默认归因于人；
2. 让 HR 流程脱离真实经营结果。

---

## AI-native extension / AI 原生扩展

AI and Agents change the allocation of **work**, but they do not eliminate the need to connect work and accountability.

AI 与 Agent 改变的是**工作如何被分配**，但不会消除“工作—责任”之间的连接要求。

Extend the operating spine as:

```text
Strategy
→ Work
→ Tasks
→ Decisions
→ Accountability
→ Human | AI | Agent allocation
→ New workflow
→ New role boundaries
→ New authority
→ New metrics
→ Review
```

The key question is not “how many people can be removed?” but “what operating design produces a better business outcome with a defensible allocation of human and machine work?”.

关键问题不是“能少多少人”，而是“什么样的经营设计，能以可解释的人机分工获得更好的业务结果”。

---

## Review questions / 复盘问题

At every major review, ask both tracks:

- What changed in the business? / 业务发生了什么变化？
- What does that imply for organization and capabilities? / 对组织与能力意味着什么？
- Which responsibilities or decision rights are no longer fit? / 哪些职责或决策权已经不匹配？
- Which talent should be invested in, moved, coached, or replaced? / 哪些人才应该投资、调动、辅导或更换？
- Which mechanisms are producing unintended behavior? / 哪些机制正在制造非预期行为？
- What should be institutionalized as an organizational asset? / 哪些经验应该沉淀成组织资产？
