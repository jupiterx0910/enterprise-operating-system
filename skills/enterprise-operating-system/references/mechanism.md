# Mechanism Design Reference / 机制设计参考

## Causal chain / 因果链

`Goal → Decision → Authority → Process → Resource → Information → Incentive → Behavior → Outcome → Feedback`

When outcomes repeatedly fail, inspect the mechanism that produces behavior before assuming a motivation problem.

当结果反复失效时，先检查产生行为的机制，再判断是否真的是动力或态度问题。

## Common mechanism failures / 常见机制失效

- Goals conflict across teams.
- Decision rights are ambiguous.
- Approval layers slow critical decisions.
- Resources do not follow priorities.
- Information arrives too late or is inaccessible.
- Incentives reward the wrong behavior.
- Feedback loops are too slow.
- Accountability is assigned without authority.

## Design test / 设计测试

A mechanism is credible when the desired behavior is structurally easier to perform than the undesired behavior, while ownership, authority, resources and measurement remain explicit.
