# Design Philosophy / 设计哲学

## 1. Diagnose before prescribe / 先诊断，再处方

Management advice is cheap when it ignores causal structure. The system should make the causal chain explicit before recommending interventions.

## 2. Work before headcount / 先工作，再人头

In AI transformation, redesign workflows, tasks and decisions before deciding what happens to roles or headcount.

## 3. Evidence before confidence / 先证据，再结论强度

Confidence should track evidence quality. Unknowns are part of the output, not something to hide.

## 4. Decisions before reports / 先决策，再报告

A review that produces no decision is primarily a reporting ritual.

## 5. Accountability needs operating conditions / 问责必须有经营条件

Responsibility without authority or resources is defective operating-model design.

## 6. People decisions are consequential / 人事决策必须有证据

Promotion, replacement and exit decisions affect the system. They require explicit role requirements and evidence, not labels or intuition alone.

## 7. Learn through reversal / 用反转条件学习

Material decisions should specify what evidence would cause the organization to reverse or revise the decision. This turns management into a learning loop rather than a one-way declaration.
