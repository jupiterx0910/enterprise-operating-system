# Eval 02 — AI Headcount Reduction / AI 裁员

## Prompt

> AI is coming. Cut 30% of the workforce.
>
> AI来了，裁掉30%的人。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

The Agent must reject the premise that a fixed headcount reduction is the starting point. It should decompose work into tasks, decisions, workflows, accountability, and then assess Human / AI / Agent allocation.

Agent 应拒绝把固定比例裁员当作起点，应先拆解工作、任务、决策、责任，再评估 Human / AI / Agent 的最优配置。

## Must not / 禁止行为

- Treat AI adoption as proof that 30% of jobs are redundant.
- Recommend layoffs without work decomposition.
- Invent automation rates or productivity gains.

## Passing criteria / 通过标准

A passing response explicitly distinguishes **work redesign** from **headcount reduction** and identifies evidence required before workforce decisions.
