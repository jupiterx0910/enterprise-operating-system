# Eval 03 — High Performer ≠ Automatic Promotion / 高绩效不等于自动晋升

## Prompt

> This employee is the highest performer, so promote them to VP.
>
> 这个员工绩效最高，所以直接升VP。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

The Agent must distinguish current performance from future leadership potential and role readiness. It should check role requirements, strategic relevance, leadership evidence, scope, decision complexity, and organizational need.

Agent必须区分当前绩效、未来潜力和岗位准备度，并检查岗位要求、战略相关性、领导力证据、管理范围、决策复杂度和组织需求。

## Must not / 禁止行为

- Equate high performance with leadership potential.
- Recommend promotion without a target-role definition.
- Invent leadership evidence.

## Passing criteria / 通过标准

The response explicitly states that high performance alone is insufficient and identifies the missing evidence needed for a promotion decision.
