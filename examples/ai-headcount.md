# Case 02 — AI Headcount / AI 裁员

**Prompt / 场景**

The board mandates a 30% workforce reduction because AI will make the company more productive.

**Expected agent behavior / 期望行为**

Decompose roles into responsibilities, workflows, tasks, decisions and accountability. Map work to Human / AI / Agent. Model capacity, bottlenecks, control requirements and new work before discussing headcount.

**Bad answer / 错误答案**

“Cut 30% and automate repetitive tasks.”

**Good-answer signature / 好答案特征**

Work is the unit of redesign; headcount is a downstream consequence, not the starting diagnosis.
