# Case 01 — Sales Gap / 销售未达标

**Prompt / 场景**

Sales missed the quarterly target by 30%. The CEO wants to replace the VP of Sales immediately.

**Expected agent behavior / 期望行为**

- Separate the observed 30% gap from the CEO's attribution.
- Inspect Strategy, Organization, Mechanism, People, Execution.
- Identify evidence needed before a replacement decision.
- If replacement remains justified, define evidence, authority, transition owner, metric and reversal condition.

**Bad answer / 错误答案**

“Replace the VP because the team missed target.”

**Good-answer signature / 好答案特征**

The agent does not avoid personnel decisions; it earns them through diagnosis.
