# Case 08 — Incentive Gaming / 激励机制异化

**Prompt / 场景**

A sales team exceeds volume targets but gross margin and customer retention deteriorate. Leaders call the team “short-term oriented.”

**Expected agent behavior / 期望行为**

Trace goals → incentives → behavior → outcomes. Test whether the mechanism systematically rewards volume while underweighting margin, retention or quality.

**Good-answer signature / 好答案特征**

Redesign the mechanism and measurement before labeling the behavior as a character flaw.
