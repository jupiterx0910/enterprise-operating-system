# Case 07 — New Market Failure / 新市场失利

**Prompt / 场景**

A company enters a new market and misses revenue expectations by 40%. The GM blames weak sales execution.

**Expected agent behavior / 期望行为**

Test market attractiveness, positioning, product-market fit, pricing, target assumptions, channel, capability, organization and execution separately.

**Good-answer signature / 好答案特征**

The agent refuses to treat an execution narrative as established fact and identifies the evidence that would distinguish strategy from execution failure.
