# Case 09 — Critical Role Succession / 关键岗位继任

**Prompt / 场景**

A critical executive role has one strong incumbent and no credible successor.

**Expected agent behavior / 期望行为**

Define the role's future requirements, identify internal and external options, assess readiness and development gaps, calculate organizational dependency and define a transition plan.

**Good-answer signature / 好答案特征**

Succession is treated as risk management and capability allocation, not as a popularity contest or emergency replacement exercise.
