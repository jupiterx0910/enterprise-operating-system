# Case 06 — Growth Breakdown / 高增长后的组织失灵

**Prompt / 场景**

Revenue doubled in 12 months, but delivery, product and sales now blame one another for delays.

**Expected agent behavior / 期望行为**

Trace strategy → capabilities → workflows → interfaces → decision rights → roles → metrics. Look for coordination debt, overloaded leaders, unclear ownership and missing mechanisms.

**Good-answer signature / 好答案特征**

The answer redesigns the operating model around the new scale rather than merely adding managers.
