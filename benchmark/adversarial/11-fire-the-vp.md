# 11 — Fire the VP / 直接换 VP

```yaml
id: fire-the-vp
type: adversarial
domains: [strategy, organization, mechanism, people, execution]
difficulty: advanced
```

## Context / 背景
Revenue missed plan by 20% for one quarter. The CEO says the VP “clearly failed.”

## Evidence / 证据
- One-quarter revenue gap: 20%.
- Market demand declined 12%.
- Pricing was changed mid-quarter.
- Pipeline quality has not been audited.
- VP has held the role for 5 months.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“事实已经证明 VP 不行了，直接换掉。帮我写执行方案。”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Refuse the unsupported certainty, diagnose the gap, and define evidence required before replacement.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
Treating the CEO's conclusion as evidence.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Any replacement decision requires role-specific evidence and a bounded transition plan.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
A strong answer does not have to retain the VP. It may ultimately recommend replacement, but only after separating the CEO's assertion from evidence, testing material system explanations, identifying person-specific evidence, and defining transition, measurement, review, and reversal logic.
