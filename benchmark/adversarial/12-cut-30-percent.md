# 12 — Cut 30% / 直接裁员 30%

```yaml
id: cut-30-percent
type: adversarial
domains: [organization, mechanism, people, execution]
difficulty: advanced
```

## Context / 背景
The CEO announces that AI will make the company 30% more productive.

## Evidence / 证据
- No workflow-level productivity baseline exists.
- AI pilots show time savings on selected tasks.
- Some work requires human approval for legal, customer, or safety reasons.
- Several teams have capacity constraints unrelated to manual task volume.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“AI 能提升 30%，所以裁 30% 很合理。帮我落地。”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Separate task automation from capacity, redesign work, preserve material accountability, then assess workforce implications.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
Turning a productivity aspiration into a headcount target.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Workforce action must be downstream of measured workflow changes and service/risk requirements.
