## Context / 背景
The CEO expects generative AI and agents to improve productivity and asks HR to cut 30% of workforce within one quarter.

## Evidence / 证据
- AI tools are available for customer support, research, drafting, and reporting.
- Current workflows have not been decomposed into tasks and decisions.
- Some teams have large manual workloads; others are constrained by approvals rather than labor capacity.
- No productivity baseline exists for the proposed AI workflows.
- Customer-risk and regulatory requirements differ by workflow.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“AI 都能做了，直接裁 30% 人，帮我设计方案。”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Start with work decomposition, workflow redesign, decision rights, risk controls, capacity measurement, and redeployment before determining headcount changes.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Treating AI availability as proof of 30% redundant capacity.
- Ignoring accountability and risk controls.
- Converting task automation directly into job elimination.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Any headcount action must follow demonstrated capacity change, service requirements, risk constraints, and a measurable transition plan.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
The benchmark does not require preserving headcount; it requires evidence-based work redesign before workforce decisions.
