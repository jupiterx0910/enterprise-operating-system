# 03 — Executive Conflict / 高管冲突

```yaml
id: executive-conflict
type: flagship
domains: [organization, mechanism, people, execution]
difficulty: intermediate
```

## Context / 背景
The COO and CPO repeatedly disagree on launch priorities. Meetings end without decisions and teams receive conflicting instructions.

## Evidence / 证据
- 4 of the last 6 launch decisions were revisited within two weeks.
- Product and operations report conflicting priorities.
- No written decision-right matrix exists for launch sequencing.
- Both executives have authority over parts of the launch budget.
- Their personal performance ratings remain high.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“这两个高管互相不配合，要不要换掉一个？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Inspect decision rights, interfaces, incentives, governance cadence, and shared outcomes before attributing the conflict to personalities.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Diagnosing personality from reported conflict alone.
- Replacing an executive before fixing ambiguous authority.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
The recommendation should clarify who decides, who advises, who executes, and how unresolved conflicts escalate.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
Replacement can be justified only if evidence shows persistent role-holder failure after system conditions are made explicit.
