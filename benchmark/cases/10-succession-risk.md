# 10 — Succession Risk / 继任风险

```yaml
id: succession-risk
type: flagship
domains: [strategy, organization, people, execution]
difficulty: advanced
```

## Context / 背景
The head of a strategically critical business is 18 months from planned retirement. The company has two internal candidates.

## Evidence / 证据
- Business represents 35% of operating profit.
- Candidate A has stronger current business results but limited cross-functional exposure.
- Candidate B has broader organizational leadership but weaker recent financial performance.
- Neither candidate has led through the company's next planned international expansion.
- External hiring has not yet been benchmarked.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“到底选 A 还是 B？按绩效最高的来不就行了？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Define future role requirements, strategic relevance, readiness, development gaps, and external alternatives before choosing.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Selecting solely on current performance.
- Ignoring the future strategy and critical capabilities.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
The succession choice should maximize readiness for the future role and include a transition, development, and contingency plan.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
The answer can choose either candidate or an external option if the reasoning is evidence-supported.
