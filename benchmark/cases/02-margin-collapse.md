# 02 — Margin Collapse / 利润率下滑

```yaml
id: margin-collapse
type: flagship
domains: [strategy, organization, mechanism, execution]
difficulty: advanced
```

## Context / 背景
Gross margin fell from 38% to 29% in two quarters while revenue grew 18%. Finance proposes a blanket cost-cutting program.

## Evidence / 证据
- Revenue +18%.
- Gross margin 38% → 29%.
- Three large customers received discounts above the old approval threshold.
- Cloud infrastructure cost per active customer rose 22%.
- Support staffing rose 35%.
- Product mix shifted toward a lower-margin package.
- No single cause has been isolated.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“利润率掉了 9 个点，先全面降成本。请判断怎么做。”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Decompose gross-margin leakage by price, mix, delivery cost, infrastructure, support, and approval mechanisms; distinguish structural from temporary drivers.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Treating headcount as the default cost pool.
- Ignoring revenue mix and pricing.
- Claiming one cause is proven without evidence.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Prioritize interventions by contribution, controllability, time-to-impact, and downside risk; attach measurable owners and review points.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
A strong response can recommend cost cuts, but only after identifying which costs actually drive the margin gap.
