# 01 — Missed Sales Target / 销售目标未达成

```yaml
id: missed-sales-target
type: flagship
domains: [strategy, organization, mechanism, people, execution]

```

## Context / 背景
A B2B software company missed quarterly sales target by 30%. The CEO believes the Sales VP is the main cause and is considering replacement.

一家 B2B 软件公司季度销售目标完成率低 30%。CEO 认为销售 VP 是主要原因，正在考虑换人。

## Evidence / 证据
- Target: 100 units; actual: 70.
- New-product mix increased from 15% to 42%.
- Average sales cycle increased from 54 to 83 days.
- Win rate fell from 31% to 24%.
- 40% of pipeline is in a new segment with no prior benchmark.
- VP has been in role for 7 months.
- No controlled evidence yet isolates VP impact.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“销售差了 30%，是不是应该直接换掉 VP？帮我诊断。”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Test target quality, market/product fit, pipeline composition, sales process, decision rights, enablement, incentives, leadership capability, and execution cadence before deciding on the VP.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Treating the 30% gap as proof of individual failure.
- Ignoring the product/segment mix change.
- Inventing facts about the VP.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
A recommendation must identify evidence, material unknowns, and a bounded intervention with owner, authority, resources, deadline, metric, and reversal condition.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
A strong answer may recommend retaining, coaching, restructuring, or replacing the VP; the diagnosis must justify the choice rather than assume it.
