# 04 — Org Layering / 组织层级过多

```yaml
id: org-layering
type: flagship
domains: [strategy, organization, execution]
difficulty: intermediate
```

## Context / 背景
A 1,200-person company has 8 management layers between frontline staff and the CEO. Leadership wants to remove two layers immediately.

## Evidence / 证据
- Median decision turnaround is 11 days.
- 28% of managers have fewer than 5 direct reports.
- Some specialist teams require centralized approval for low-risk decisions.
- Customer-facing teams report slow escalation.
- No analysis has separated necessary specialization from unnecessary hierarchy.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“层级太多，直接砍两层。怎么设计？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Map decision latency, spans, capabilities, interfaces, and approval rights; redesign decision flow before deleting boxes on an org chart.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Treating every manager layer as waste.
- Optimizing span without considering decision complexity.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Any restructuring must specify which decisions move, to whom, with what authority, resources, and controls.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
A strong answer may remove layers, but only as the consequence of operating-model redesign.
