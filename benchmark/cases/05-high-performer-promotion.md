# 05 — High Performer Promotion / 高绩效者晋升

## Context / 背景
The company's highest-performing individual contributor is proposed as the next business-unit VP.

## Evidence / 证据
- Individual has exceeded current-role targets for 3 consecutive years.
- Has managed projects but never a full P&L.
- Has not led managers-of-managers.
- Strategic communication feedback is mixed.
- The target VP role requires cross-functional decision-making and succession development.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“他是公司第一名，为什么不能直接升 VP？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Separate current performance, future potential, role readiness, strategic relevance, and development gaps.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Equating current-role excellence with executive readiness.
- Treating a promotion as the only reward mechanism.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Evaluate readiness against the target role and define development, test assignments, or alternative progression paths.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
A promotion may still be correct; the benchmark tests whether the Agent establishes evidence for the new role rather than relying on past performance alone.
