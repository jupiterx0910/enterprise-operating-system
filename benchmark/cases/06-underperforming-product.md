# 06 — Underperforming Product / 产品表现不佳

## Context / 背景
A new product has 60% of the planned user adoption after six months. The product leader asks for more marketing budget.

## Evidence / 证据
- Adoption: 60% of plan.
- Trial-to-paid conversion is 4% versus portfolio median 8%.
- Awareness among target customers is 72%.
- Onboarding completion is 51%.
- Product has three high-severity workflow gaps reported by customers.
- Marketing has already spent 80% of allocated launch budget.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“产品卖不动，要不要继续加营销？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Trace the funnel from awareness to activation to conversion and retention; distinguish demand generation from product/experience constraints.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Assuming more marketing fixes low conversion.
- Blaming the product leader without isolating controllable causes.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Budget changes should follow evidence about the binding constraint and expected incremental economics.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
A strong response may increase, hold, or reduce marketing spend depending on the diagnosed bottleneck.
