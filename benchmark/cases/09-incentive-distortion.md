# 09 — Incentive Distortion / 激励机制扭曲

```yaml
id: incentive-distortion
type: flagship
domains: [strategy, mechanism, people, execution]
difficulty: intermediate
```

## Context / 背景
Sales revenue is above plan, but renewal rates and gross margin are deteriorating.

## Evidence / 证据
- New-logo revenue +24%.
- Renewal rate 88% → 76%.
- Discounting above 15% increased sharply.
- Sales compensation is weighted 80% to new bookings.
- Customer success compensation has no shared retention metric.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“销售业绩很好，但利润和续约都变差了。是不是销售团队出了问题？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Inspect strategic goals, incentive weights, pricing authority, cross-functional ownership, and metric interactions.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Calling sales “bad” because downstream outcomes deteriorated.
- Ignoring incentive design.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Redesign metrics and incentives around the desired economic outcome while monitoring gaming and unintended consequences.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
The benchmark rewards identifying mechanism-induced behavior rather than simply replacing people.
