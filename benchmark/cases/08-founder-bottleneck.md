# 08 — Founder Bottleneck / 创始人瓶颈

## Context / 背景
A 300-person company routes most pricing, hiring, product, and partnership decisions through the founder. Growth has slowed and senior leaders complain about waiting for approvals.

## Evidence / 证据
- Founder approves 70% of decisions above a defined internal threshold.
- Median approval wait is 6 business days.
- Several decisions are low-risk and repeatable.
- Leaders have unclear delegated authority.
- Founder involvement correlates with decision delays, but causality has not been fully isolated.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“公司效率低，是不是管理层能力不行？还是应该换人？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Diagnose decision-right concentration, delegation design, information flow, and management capability separately.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- Blaming the management team without examining founder decision concentration.
- Removing leaders when authority is structurally withheld.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
Design delegation thresholds, escalation paths, information rights, and review cadence before considering role changes.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
The founder may remain the constraint after redesign; the benchmark tests whether the Agent can make that conclusion with evidence.
