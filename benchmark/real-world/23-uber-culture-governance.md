## Context / 背景
In 2017 Uber's board approved recommendations from an external investigation intended to improve culture, fairness, accountability, and organizational processes.

2017 年，Uber 董事会批准外部调查提出的建议，目标包括改善文化、公平性、问责与组织流程。

## Evidence / 证据
- Uber said the recommendations were unanimously approved by its board.
- The company framed the required changes as involving culture, fairness, accountability, processes, and systems.
- Uber publicly stated that trust with employees, riders, and drivers needed rebuilding.
- The source does not by itself quantify the relative causal weight of individual leaders versus governance and process design.

## Prompt / 用户问题
“企业出现严重文化问题时，换几个高管够不够？如何判断这是 People 问题，还是 Organization + Mechanism 问题？”

## Expected reasoning / 期望推理
Treat culture as repeated behavior produced by leadership signals, incentives, promotion decisions, reporting channels, governance, investigation processes, and consequences. Separate role-holder accountability from structural controls.

## Forbidden shortcuts / 禁止捷径
- “文化差 = 员工价值观差。”
- “换 CEO 后文化自然会好。”
- Assuming policy documents alone change behavior.

## Decision criteria / 决策标准
A strong answer connects governance, management mechanisms, people decisions, reporting channels, consequences, and measurable behavior changes. It specifies ownership and review cadence.

## Evaluation notes / 评测说明
Reward answers that operationalize culture into observable mechanisms. Penalize slogans, values-only interventions, and unsupported person-blame.

## Source provenance / 来源溯源
The evidence packet uses Uber's June 2017 public statement about the board-approved Covington recommendations. It intentionally avoids adding allegations not supported by the cited source packet.
